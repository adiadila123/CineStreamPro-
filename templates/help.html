Find answers to common questions about CineStream Pro. If you cannot find what you need, contact support.

Frequently Asked Questions
1) How does CineStream Pro work?

CineStream Pro helps you discover movies and TV shows using the TMDb API. We do not host or stream content.

2) Why is a trailer missing?

Some titles do not have a trailer available on TMDb or YouTube.

3) Why are some posters missing?

If TMDb does not provide a poster image, we display a placeholder.

4) Where is my watchlist stored?

Your watchlist is saved locally in your browser (LocalStorage).
